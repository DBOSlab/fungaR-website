fungaR is an R package for accessing, analyzing, and curating fungal taxonomic and distributional data from the Flora e Funga do Brasil (FFB) platform, maintained by the Rio de Janeiro Botanical Garden. It provides a comprehensive interface to download, parse, filter, and explore checklist data from the FFB IPT data portal — from browsing by taxonomic, geographic, or trait criteria to resolving your own species name lists against it, and cross-checking MycoBank and Brazilian occurrence repositories to help curate species and records still missing from the platform.

Flora e Funga do Brasil

Brazil harbors one of the richest fungal diversities in the world, spanning lichenized and non-lichenized fungi across every biome in the country. Assembling a complete, continuously updated checklist of this diversity has been a decades-long collaborative effort involving hundreds of taxonomists and mycologists across the country.

The project began in 2008 as the Lista de Espécies da Flora do Brasil, a first attempt to compile a national species checklist validated by specialists. It evolved into Flora do Brasil 2020, launched to meet Brazil’s commitments under the Global Strategy for Plant Conservation of the Convention on Biological Diversity, and — following the incorporation of fungi — into its current form, Flora e Funga do Brasil.

For each accepted fungal name, the checklist records nomenclatural status (accepted, synonym, or doubtful), taxonomic classification (Division, Order, Family, Genus — FFB does not use Class for Fungi), geographic distribution across Brazilian states and phytogeographic domains, endemism status, nutritional/ecological mode (e.g. saprobic, parasitic, lichenized, mycorrhizal), and substrate/host information. This structured, validated backbone underpins mycological research, conservation assessments, and biodiversity informatics across the country.

Data are published as versioned Darwin Core Archives through the FFB IPT Data Portal, hosted by the Rio de Janeiro Botanical Garden (JBRJ). fungaR reads directly from this portal, so every result reflects the checklist as curated by the taxonomist community at the time of the query.

If you are a taxonomist and wish to contribute new fungal names, updates, or corrections to Flora e Funga do Brasil, see the contribution guidelines on the platform itself, or contact the FFB coordination team at floraefungadobrasil@jbrj.gov.br. fungaR’s funga_mycobank_gap() and funga_distribution_gap() functions can help you find candidates worth submitting.

```{r setup, include=FALSE}
library(fungaR)

ffb_versions <- tryCatch(
  funga_version(),
  error = function(e) NULL
)

if (!is.null(ffb_versions) && nrow(ffb_versions) > 0) {
  latest_row     <- ffb_versions[ffb_versions$Latest %in% TRUE, ]
  if (nrow(latest_row) == 0) latest_row <- ffb_versions[1, ]
  n_versions     <- nrow(ffb_versions)
  latest_version <- latest_row$Version[1]
  latest_records <- format(as.numeric(gsub(",", "", latest_row$Records[1])), big.mark = ",")
  latest_date    <- latest_row$Published_on[1]
} else {
  n_versions     <- NA
  latest_version <- NA
  latest_records <- NA
  latest_date    <- NA
}
```
